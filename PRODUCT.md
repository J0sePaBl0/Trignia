# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Clinic owners/directors and operations/administration managers at clinics and wellness centers. Owners decide; ops managers influence and evaluate. They arrive wanting to know whether Trignia can fix their operational pain (manual tasks, appointment handling, patient follow-up) and to book a meeting.

## Product Purpose
Trignia is a consultancy/service that combines automation, artificial intelligence and analytics for clinics and wellness centers, helping them lead their digital transformation. The site exists to get visitors to book a meeting or free consultation. Success is a booked meeting, made on the `/agendar` page; the contact form and hola@trignia.com remain for general messages.

## Positioning
Fully custom solutions: Trignia analyzes each clinic's operation, designs intelligent flows around it, and accompanies the clinic through its digital evolution, rather than selling a one-size-fits-all product.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Stack: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS 4, motion (Framer Motion), Spline; existing sections: Hero, Problems, Steps, Why Trignia, Testimonials (hidden), Contact form, Chat widget; separate `/agendar` booking page.
- Site copy is currently in Spanish.
- Primary CTA: "Agendar una reunión" / free consultation. Every scheduling button on the landing links to `/agendar`.
- The `/agendar` page runs on example availability and does not record bookings until its backend is built.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the existing logo, brand assets and emerald/green primary color. Current headline voice: "LA NUEVA ERA de la Gestión CLÍNICA".

## Evidence on Hand
No real testimonials, client names, results or statistics exist yet. The Testimonials section is placeholder content and is hidden. Do not invent clients, quotes, metrics or logos.

## Product Principles
- Personalization over packaged product: every claim should reflect tailoring to the clinic.
- One clear action: booking a meeting stays the primary path on every surface.
- Credibility without fabricated proof: earn trust through specificity and process, not invented results.
- Speak to both decision maker and operator.
